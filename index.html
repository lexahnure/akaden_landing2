5 steps to turn request into pipeline

Akaden combines integration development, AI assistance, environment access, testing, deployment, and operational tooling inside the same project workspace.

01

Start with the real request

Bring in the business need, institutional context, and constraints. Akaden starts with what your team knows instead of asking a developer to begin from a blank prompt.

02

Build a plan with the right skills

Akaden automatically selects the planning and design skills the task needs. It applies established practices, explores the missing decisions, and asks the questions that make the plan complete.

03

Keep the decision with your team

Your team edits and approves the plan. This is where business priorities, technical judgment, and institutional knowledge stay firmly in human hands.

04

Build from an approved blueprint

Akaden brings pipeline-building skills to the approved plan, creating an implementation that developers can inspect, understand, and update.

05

Test, deploy, and learn

Set the runtime values, deploy to the right tenant, and validate the output against the agreed acceptance criteria. The next change starts from the same shared context.
